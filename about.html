About me

I moved into software after eight years of designing piping systems for oil and gas plants.

Background

I'm a computer science student at the University of Alberta, graduating in April 2027 with a minor in Spanish.

Before computer science, I earned a diploma in Engineering Design and Drafting Technology from NAIT and worked for eight years as a piping designer at Worley in Edmonton. I made piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs), line lists and isometric drawings, and put together construction packages for maintenance outages at the Suncor Edmonton Refinery.

Most of my projects are in Python. The largest is Munch, a federated social network built with Django and Django REST Framework. I also make small games and Minecraft mods for fun.

What I'm looking for

A new grad backend software engineering role starting in 2027. I'm most interested in startups and Alberta tech companies, and I'm open to remote work or relocating from Edmonton. Longer term, I want to work on machine learning.
